١الفجوة في جانب الجمعيات، لا في جانب المتبرعين

نُشر للشرقية ما قيمته ٢٣ ريالاً لكل ساكن مقابل ١٩٧ ريالاً للباحة، وهي دون حصتها العادلة في كل المجالات. ومع ذلك تُموَّل معظم فرصها بسرعة قريبة من المعدل الوطني. السبب في عدد الجمعيات: لدى الشرقية ٠٫٤٣× من المعدل الوطني من الجمعيات الشريكة لكل ساكن، ولا ينشر مشاريع منها إلا ٠٫٧٥× من النسبة الوطنية، مع أن من ينشر منها أنشط من المتوسط (١٫٢٣×). والرياض كذلك قليلة الجمعيات (٠٫٥١×)، وتبوك تنشر فرصاً أصغر (٠٫٨٢×).

٢التوقيت يغيّر سرعة التمويل

شعبان أبطأ شهر في التمويل، دون متوسط سنته في ٥ من ٥ سنوات (−١٤ نقطة)، و٤٠٪ من فرصه لا تكتمل إلا في رمضان. أما رمضان فأسرع من متوسط سنته (+٧ نقاط) رغم أنه يستقبل ٤ أضعاف المنشور. ويبلغ العطاء ذروته يوم ٢٧ (يوم الرواتب، ١٫٦× يوم عادي) ويوم الجمعة (١٫٥×).

٣ما الذي يستجيب له المتبرعون؟

بُني نموذج يقدّر، يوم النشر، احتمال أن يتأخر التمويل أكثر من أسبوع؛ والغرض فهم سلوك المتبرعين لا تشغيله على المنصة. أقوى العوامل المبلغ وازدحام النشر: الأسابيع المزدحمة (رمضان خاصة) أسرع لا أبطأ. أما المنطقة فلا تضيف شيئاً، فالمتبرعون لا يفرّقون بين المناطق. والنموذج متواضع بصدق (AUC ٠٫٧٠): معظم البطء لا يظهر يوم النشر.

Where should the next riyal go?

Which region × cause pairs should come first for grants and support to associations? An analysis of every donation opportunity in the Projects area of Ehsan, Saudi Arabia's national giving platform, February 2021 – October 2026.

The answer in brief: the next riyal should go first to the Eastern Region, which holds 6 of the 15 priority pairs, then Riyadh (4), Makkah (2), Madinah (2) and Jazan (1). In 12 pairs donors fund what is posted at a normal pace, so the gap is how little is posted, not donors; the best use of money is associations' capacity to prepare and post opportunities. Only 3 pairs are both under-posted and slow to fund, and need grants or matching funds: Eastern feeding, Eastern housing and Jazan religious programmes.

1The gap is on the association side, not the donor side

Projects worth SAR 23 per resident were posted for the Eastern Region, against SAR 197 for Al-Bahah; Eastern is below its fair share in every cause. Yet most of its opportunities fund at close to the national pace. The difference is in associations: Eastern has 0.43× the national number of partner associations per resident, and only 0.75× the national share of them post projects, although those that post are more active than average (1.23×). Riyadh also has few associations (0.51×), and Tabuk posts smaller opportunities (0.82×).

2Timing changes how fast donors fund

Sha'ban is the slowest month to fund, below its year's average in 5 of 5 years (−14 points), and 40% of its opportunities complete only in Ramadan. Ramadan funds faster than its year (+7 points) despite 4× the postings. Giving peaks on the 27th (salary day, 1.6× an average day) and on Fridays (1.5×).

3What donors respond to

A model estimates, on the day of posting, the chance that funding takes more than a week; its purpose is to understand donors, not to be run by a platform. The strongest features are amount and competition: busy weeks (above all Ramadan) fund faster, not slower. Region adds nothing: donors do not treat regions differently. The model is honestly modest (AUC 0.70): most slowness is not visible on posting day.

Method and responsible use of AI

  • Data: Ehsan's public pages (collected politely: 1.5 s between requests, every page cached), the GASTAT 2022 census, and partner-association counts from Ehsan's statistics page. No personal data: titles of individual cases were dropped at collection; all analysis is aggregated. NCNP: National Center for Non-Profit Sector.
  • Documented cleaning: every step is in a cleaning log and data dictionary; region was filled for 1,823 opportunities (3 remain unknown), and Ehsan's first months were left out of speed comparisons.
  • Fair model testing: trained on the past, tested on the latest 15 months. A random split would have shown a misleading 0.82. Two tempting results were rejected: a signal caused by a change in report-page layout, and a bigger model that won only on the test.
  • Limits: Ehsan project opportunities only; patterns, not proven causes. AI use is documented in ai_usage_log.md.
